# The Alchemist's Celestial Laboratory — Spot the Differences

An interactive micro-observation game specifically crafted for students to develop deep visual inspection, patience, and analytical comparison skills.

---

## 🎯 Game Rules & Mechanics

### 1. The 13-Click Focus Rule
- Students have **13 Focus Charges (clicks)** to discover **10 differences**.
- Every click on either scene consumes 1 Focus Charge (whether correct or false).
- This leaves room for **at most 3 mistakes**.
- Fast clicking or guessing is strictly penalized.

### 2. Mandatory 2-Minute Reflection Lockout
- If a student depletes all 13 Focus Charges without uncovering all 10 differences, the investigation automatically enters a **mandatory 2-minute cooling lockout** (`02:00` live countdown).
- The retry and reset buttons are locked during this period.
- Students are prompted to observe the static twin scenes calmly in stillness, reflect on their misclicks, and plan their strategy before their next attempt.
- Alternatively, students/teachers can choose to surrender the case and inspect the solution.

### 3. Synchronized Magnifying Loupe (2.5x Zoom)
- Because the 10 differences are subtle micro-details (hairline tick marks, tiny companion stars, knot counts, and math notation), using the magnifying loupe is essential!
- Hovering or dragging across either image renders dual synchronized 2.5x circular lenses on both scenes simultaneously.

### 4. Non-Spatial Numbering Order
- Differences are **not numbered in spatial or sequential order** across the canvas.
- As students find differences, markers are badged with a discovery checkmark (`✓ #1`, `✓ #2`, etc.) in the order they were discovered.
- The Detective Observation Log lists discoveries chronologically as they are solved.
